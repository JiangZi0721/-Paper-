### 1. [RSI-Anything: Unlocking Recursive Self-Improvement with World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13912)

- **论文元数据**：`arXiv:2610.13912` | [Hugging Face 论文讨论页](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.13912) | [arXiv 原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13912) | [💻 官方开源代码](https://github.com/Gen-Verse/RSI-Anything)
- **官方 PDF 文献**：**[📑 点击在线阅读官方原版 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13912.pdf)**
- **学术评级**：⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 【通用预测世界模型驱动 RSI · 策略-模型对兼容性门控 · 五代持续翻倍】
- **双维定位**：`[慢思考推理与隐式世界模型 (Reasoning / World Model)]` × `[测试时/运行态自演进 (Runtime Evolution)]`
- **研究团队与机构**：Taoyong Cui, Pinxian Zeng, Wanghan Xu, Hao Xiao, Shuhan Xue 等（PhAI Labs / 上海人工智能实验室生态）
- **核心自进化流派**：模型基递归自我提升（Model-Based RSI）、策略与预测世界模型协同演化（Policy-WorldModel Co-Evolution）

#### 🔬 深度科研结构化拆解：
1. **具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：
   研究在真实物理/计算环境外部反馈极其昂贵的约束下，如何利用联合嵌入预测架构（`JEPA-Anything`）构建通用预测世界模型，引导动作选择与合成学习数据筛选，并让世界模型与策略/工作流在多轮迭代中协同递归自进化。
2. **解决了什么核心痛点 (Problem Solved & Pain Points)**：
   - **外部真实交互预算受限**：纯靠外部环境真实执行来筛选候选动作或工作流，样本开销极大；
   - **策略演化诱发的世界模型失配（Model Drift under Policy Shift）**：随着智能体策略 $\theta_k$ 的不断演进，其访问的状态-动作分布发生偏移，静态世界模型预测迅速失准，甚至给出误导性虚假高分。
3. **提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
   提出 **RSI-Anything** 领域通用递归自提升框架，在第 $k$ 轮演化周期维护系统对 $(\theta_k, \phi_{d,k})$：
   - **正交预测分解世界模型（OPF World Model）**：基于 `JEPA-Anything`，通过编码器 $z_t = E_{\phi_{d,k}}(s_t, c_d)$ 与正交因子预测器 $\widehat{z}_{t+1} = F_{\phi_{d,k}}(z_t, a_t, c_d)$ 预测未来潜在表征，并映射为收益读出 $\widehat{o}_{t+1} = R_{\phi_{d,k}}(\widehat{z}_{t+1}, c_d)$；
   - **不确定性惩罚的预测引导选择**：对策略提议的候选集 $\mathcal{K}_t = \operatorname{Propose}_{\theta_k}(s_t, g)$，按惩罚后效用打分筛选执行：
     $$R_d(\widehat{\tau}) = \sum_j w_{d,j} r_{\tau,j} - \lambda_d U(\mathbf{u}_\tau)$$
   - **想象轨迹准入过滤（Imagined Trajectory Admission Gate）**：对世界模型生成的合成训练轨迹施加多重门控：
     $$e_\tau = \mathbf{1}\{v_\tau=1\} \, \mathbf{1}\{R_d(\widehat{\tau}) \ge r_d^{\min}\} \, \mathbf{1}\{U(\mathbf{u}_\tau) \le u_d^{\max}\} \, \mathbf{1}\{\ell_\tau \in \mathcal{E}_d^{\mathrm{train}}\}$$
   - **策略与世界模型双轨更新及兼容性门禁（Pair Compatibility Checks）**：真实反馈同时驱动策略/工作流更新 $\theta'_{k+1}$ 与世界模型自适应校准 $\phi'_{d,k+1}$，仅当组件独立校验与 $(\theta'_{k+1}, \phi'_{d,k+1})$ 联合兼容性门禁通过时才予以保留。
   - **跨领域实测突破**：在代码修复中，固定 Coder 下预测筛选+世界模型自适应将成功率从 26.3% 提升至 **31.2%**；GUI 智能体平均动作步数减少 **39.4%**；实验室规划任务经历 **5 代策略演化后得分从 21.80% 翻倍至 46.01%**；化学反应优化在 40 次实验预算下达到 **96.15%** 产率（显著超越贝叶斯优化的 82.16%）。
4. **对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
   将 Model-Based RSI 从封闭网格游戏推向了代码修复、GUI 操作、湿实验规划与免疫学工作流演化等通用科学与工程领域，确立了“策略进化必须伴随世界模型同步校准与兼容性门控”的核心准则。

> Recursive self-improvement (RSI) aims to transform interaction experience into persistent improvements, yet external feedback is costly and world-model predictions may become unreliable as policies evolve. We introduce RSI-Anything, a domain-general framework built on JEPA-Anything that uses predictive world models to guide action and learning-data selection. Observed feedback drives policy or workflow improvement and conditional world-model adaptation, while component and compatibility checks govern the retention of updated policy-model pairs. Across diverse computational and scientific domains, RSI-Anything demonstrates substantial gains. In code repair, with the coder fixed, success increases from 26.3% with a static verifier to 29.2% through predictive selection and 31.2% after world-model adaptation, at comparable test usage. Graphical user interface (GUI) evaluations showed higher task completion, while average actions decrease by 39.4%. Across five successive policy versions, laboratory-planning scores more than double from 21.80% to 46.01%. In reaction optimization, RSI-Anything achieves 96.15% mean best observed yield versus 82.16% for Bayesian optimization under the same 40-experiment budget. An end-to-end immune-science application further connects workflow evolution and predictive modeling with therapeutic hypothesis generation. These results demonstrate how predictive world models and retained system updates enable recursive improvement across diverse environments.

- **论文标题**：ReHarness: Recurrent Harness for Agentic Continual Learning
- **作者与机构**：Yuxin Xiao, Zhiyun Lu, Jianyu Wang, Yangtian Zhang, Yang Song
- **论文官方直达**：[arXiv 摘要 (`2610.12668`)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12668) | [官方 PDF 预览](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.12668.pdf) | [HF Papers 讨论](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.12668)

- **二维交叉定位**：`[外围架构与上下文演进 (Harness / Memory)]` × `[测试时/运行态自演进 (Runtime Evolution)]`

#### 🔬 四维深度学术拆解

1. **具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：
   研究在**严格在线流式协议（Strictly Online Streaming Protocol）**下，智能体面对长周期交互流与分布漂移（Distribution Shifts）时，如何通过**循环脚手架（Recurrent Harness）与反向反思修订（Backward Revision）**实现稳定性与可塑性兼具的持续学习。
2. **解决了什么核心痛点 (Problem Solved & Pain Points)**：
   指出传统有状态记忆/反思脚手架（如 Reflexion、Voyager 经验库）的致命结构缺陷——**单向前向信息流（Forward-Only Information Flow）**：
   - 传统脚手架仅有“前向写入（$\tau_{t-1} \to R_{t-1} \to h_t$）”与“前向读取（$h_t \to R_{t-1} \to \tau_t$）”；
   - 一旦早期单样本生成的反思 $R_i$ 存在过度泛化（Overgeneralization）、归因偏差或随任务分布漂移而过时，该有毒经验将永久驻留在状态 $h_t$ 中误导后续决策，且缺乏回放集或验证集来离线清洗。
3. **提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
   提出 **ReHarness**，维护状态字典 $h_t = \{(x_i, \tau_i, R_i)\}_{i=1}^{t-1}$，在每个流式样本 $x_t$ 上执行三步循环更新算子 $h_{t+1} = U(h_t, \tau_t)$：
   - **Step 1（前向读取与决策 Forward Read）**：检索 Top-$k$ 相关反思并执行决策，获得环境反馈 $r_t$ 组成当前轨迹 $\tau_t = (x_t, y_t, r_t)$：
     $$\mathcal{I}_t = \operatorname{Retrieval}(x_t, h_t, k), \quad y_t = \pi_D\big(x_t, \{R_i\}_{i \in \mathcal{I}_t}\big)$$
   - **Step 2（反向溯源修订 Backward Revision）**：核心创新！以当前下游轨迹 $\tau_t$ 作为真实性能校验信号，问出关键问题：*“当初历史样本 $i$ 的反思 $R_i$ 应当如何 differently 提炼，才能更好地指导今天的当前实例 $x_t$？”* 修订智能体 $\pi_R$ 结合源轨迹 $\tau_i$ 与当前下游轨迹 $\tau_t$，对所有被检索命中的历史反思执行**原地回溯修订（In-place Grounded Revision）**：
     $$R_i' = \pi_R(R_i, \tau_i; \tau_t), \quad \forall i \in \mathcal{I}_t$$
   - **Step 3（前向写入 Forward Write）**：记忆智能体 $\pi_M$ 对当前轨迹生成新反思 $R_t = \pi_M(\tau_t)$ 并追加至状态池。
   - 跨 3 个持续学习基准与 3 种大模型底座，ReHarness 全面超越长上下文基线、传统反射记忆库与提示词优化方法。
4. **对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
   将神经网络中的**循环误差反向传播（BPTT）思想**优雅地映射到了**纯文本脚手架状态空间（Textual Harness State Space）**——证明了持续进化的记忆库绝不能是“只增不改的静态日志堆”，而必须是能够被下游实践不断回溯纠偏的动态演化图谱。

> Language model agents increasingly operate over long interaction streams, requiring online adaptation to distribution shifts through continual learning. However, existing agent harnesses typically either evolve their states only through forward information flow or rely on additional replay or validation data for harness optimization. To address this, we propose ReHarness, a recurrent harness framework for agentic continual learning that complements forward read and write with backward revision under a strictly online streaming protocol. We ask what information could have been captured differently in the harness state to better inform the current instance in the stream. Specifically, after each instance, we leverage the resulting trajectory as feedback to revise the reflections retrieved for that instance, thereby propagating feedback backward through the textual harness state. 3. **提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
   提出 **SWE-Duel**，让两个参赛智能体 $(m, h)$（模型+脚手架组合）在真实 Docker 化开源仓库上展开镜像对称的红蓝回合（Mirrored Red-Blue Turns），构造任务元组 $C = (R, s, d, T_f, T_b)$：
   - **Red Agent 三阶段上下文隔离造题与三重可执行门禁（Three Executable Validation Gates）**：
     1. **Gate A（新功能合成）**：在真实仓库 $R$ 上提出功能规约 $s$、实现补丁并通过自写功能测试 $T_f$ 与原仓库回归测试 $T_e$；

   - **Blue Agent 盲态代码审查与修复**：Blue 仅接收 $\operatorname{pub}(C)$（甚至不知道其中必然含 Bug），提交修复补丁 $d'$，仅当三大测试集全过时获胜：
     $$S_{\mathrm{Blue}} = S_e \cdot S_f \cdot S_b = 1$$

4. **对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
   不仅是一套动态评测基准，更是**代码智能体对抗自博弈强化学习（Adversarial Self-Play RL for SWE）**的黄金任务发生器——尤其是“盲测自可解门禁（Gate C）”，为一切自博弈出题防胡编乱造提供了教科书级的工程范式。

> Static coding benchmarks are fixed at release. Over time their tasks leak into training data, and stronger agents eventually saturate them. It proposes and implements a new feature with feature tests, then embeds a subtle bug in that feature along with sealed bug-triggering tests. Claude Opus 4.8 with mini-swe-agent ranks second as the strongest task author, with a 27.3% Red win rate. Finally, within one model family, tasks written by the newest version defeat a fixed-reference solver far more often than those of older versions (42.9% vs. 4.0%), giving preliminary evidence that task difficulty scales with task authoring capability.

### 8. A²-OPD：重思在策略蒸馏中的相对策略差分迁移与全向进化 (Rethinking Relative Policy Differences Transfer of On-Policy Distillation)

- **论文标题**：Rethinking Relative Policy Differences Transfer of On-Policy Distillation
- **作者与机构**：Rong Wu, Daocheng Fu, Yu Yang, Xuemeng Yang, Siqi Li（上海人工智能实验室 KnowledgeXLab）
- **论文官方直达**：[arXiv 摘要 (`2610.13205`)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13205) | [官方 PDF 预览](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13205.pdf) | [HF Papers 讨论](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.13205)
- **开源代码仓库**：[https://github.com/KnowledgeXLab/A2-OPD](https://github.com/KnowledgeXLab/A2-OPD)
- **二维交叉定位**：`[强化学习与在策略蒸馏 (RL / OPD / Policy)]` × `[知识蒸馏阶段 (Distillation / Transfer)]`

#### 🔬 四维深度学术拆解

1. **具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：
   研究在策略蒸馏（On-Policy Distillation, OPD）中，如何打破“教师模型必须全面强于学生模型”的传统桎梏，通过迁移**代理专家策略与代理参考策略之间的相对策略差分（Relative Policy Difference）**，实现跨模型尺寸、跨训练阶段、跨代际的全向策略蒸馏（Any-to-Any OPD）。
2. **解决了什么核心痛点 (Problem Solved & Pain Points)**：
   标准 OPD 最小化逆向 KL 散度 $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{OPD}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{s \sim d_{\pi_\theta}}[D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi_\theta(\cdot \mid s) \,\|\, \pi_T(\cdot \mid s))]$，本质上是在做**绝对分布匹配（Absolute Distribution Matching）**（目标参考分布 $\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$ 被抵消）。一旦教师 $\pi_T$ 在整体规模或通用能力上弱于目标模型 $\pi_\theta$（例如用专门做过 RL 的 7B 模型去教 32B 模型），直接匹配 $\pi_T$ 的绝对分布会导致 32B 目标模型严重退化。
3. **提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
   提出统一的 **$\text{A}^2\text{-OPD}$（Any-to-Any On-Policy Distillation）** 理论与算法框架：
   - **将相对策略差分形式化为隐式 Token 级奖励**：引入一组代理策略对 $(\pi_\phi^+, \pi_\phi)$（如微调后专家 vs 微调前底座），根据逆向 RL 最优策略与奖励的对偶关系，定义对数概率比为隐式奖励：
     $$\Delta_\phi(s, a) \triangleq \log \frac{\pi_\phi^+(a \mid s)}{\pi_\phi(a \mid s)} = \frac{1}{\tau} r^*(s, a) - \log Z_\phi(s)$$
   - **全向在策略优化目标**：目标策略 $\pi_\theta$ 在自身采样轨迹 $d_{\pi_\theta}$ 上最大化该差分奖励，同时保持对自身基准锚点 $\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$（$\pi_{\mathrm{ref}} \neq \pi_\phi$）的 KL 约束：
     $$\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{A^2}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{s \sim d_{\pi_\theta}} \left[ \mathbb{E}_{a \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot \mid s)} \big[ \alpha \, \Delta_\phi(s, a) \big] - \beta D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big(\pi_\theta(\cdot \mid s) \,\|\, \pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(\cdot \mid s)\big) \right]$$
     当退化取 $\pi_\phi = \pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$ 且 $\alpha = \beta = 1$ 时，精确还原传统 OPD。
   - **揭示相对策略差分迁移的三大黄金定律（Three Empirical Laws）**：
     1. **训练增量可迁移，纯尺寸增量难迁移**：由后训练（RL 或 SFT）诱导的策略差分 $\Delta_\phi$ 能极其稳定地跨模型迁移（包括**弱教强 Weak-to-Strong** 与**老代教新代**），而单纯由模型参数规模放大（如 32B Base vs 7B Base）产生的差分几乎无法有效迁移；
     2. **专家与参考的正交互补角色**：$\pi_\phi^+$ 提供目标能力上限，而代理参考 $\pi_\phi$ 负责**滤除底座共享的通用背景行为、精准剥离出纯净的任务特定残差（Task-Specific Residual）**；
     3. **原始分布距离不决定可迁移性**：哪怕专家与目标差距悬殊，只要选用匹配的参考锚点 $\pi_\phi$ 将更新方向校准指向专家流形，就能挽救原本完全失败的跨模型蒸馏。
4. **对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
   为智能体群体协同进化（Swarm Co-Evolution）与轻量化探索（小模型低成本跑 RL 探索 $\to$ 提取 $\Delta_\phi$ 在策略无损注入巨型主力模型）奠定了坚实的数学基石！

<details><summary>👉 点击展开查看论文官方英文 Abstract 原文</summary>

> On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a target policy on its own rollouts using token-level supervision from a stronger teacher, but typically relies on the teacher being substantially more capable than the target. Recent work relaxes this requirement by transferring the relative policy difference between an expert and a reference policy rather than the expert's absolute distribution. Yet it remains unclear which differences provide useful supervision and which targets can benefit from them. We develop a unified view in which the log-probability ratio between a proxy expert and a proxy reference defines an implicit reward for KL-regularized policy optimization. This formulation induces an on-policy update for the target, recovers conventional OPD as a special case, and enables any-to-any transfer across models that differ in scale, training stage or generation. Our systematic experiments yield three findings. First, a stronger expert is not necessarily a better source of supervision: differences induced by task-specific training, including reinforcement learning and supervised fine-tuning, transfer consistently, whereas those arising from model scale alone transfer weakly or not at all. Second, the expert and reference serve complementary roles: the expert provides the capability to be transferred, while the reference filters out shared behavior and isolates the relevant residual. Third, even a useful difference does not transfer to every target: success requires the induced update to move the target toward the expert and generalize beyond sampled rollouts. Raw policy distance does not predict transferability, whereas an appropriate reference can rescue otherwise unsuccessful transfer. These results characterize when relative policy differences provide effective supervision for OPD.

2. **解决了什么核心痛点 (Problem Solved & Pain Points)**：
   近期大量实证暴露出标准在线 OPD 的三大病态伪影（Artifacts）：
   - 在线采样轨迹往往越训越长并陷入无效自我纠错死循环，直至耗尽上下文预算仍不输出 `EOS`；
   - 一旦学生模型在推理前缀中走错方向，后续长链条偏航轨迹（Misdirected Continuations）不仅浪费海量在线生成算力，更会导致教师模型在这些极度偏离自身分布的上下文上给出低质量、高噪声的不可靠监督。
3. **提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
   提出简洁高效的 **半在策略蒸馏（Semi-OPD）** 并揭示 **“师生双重在策略性（Dual On-Policyness）”** 核心定律：
   - **Semi-OPD 算法机制**：在训练开始前，仅从**初始学生模型 $\pi_{\theta_0}$** 一次性离线采样生成轨迹池并预先计算教师 Logits；在后续蒸馏过程中固定复用该轨迹池，但**每一步仍使用当前最新学生参数 $\pi_\theta$ 动态重算学生对数概率与逐 Token 优势函数（Per-token Advantage）**；
   - **横跨 17 组师生对（1.5B 至 235B）的震撼反直觉实验**：在涵盖三大模型家族、思考与非思考模式的 17 组主流师生配置中，**Semi-OPD 在多达 14 组上直接击败了昂贵的在线 OPD**，最高带来 **+13.6% 的准确率跃升** 与 **11.4 倍（$11.4\times$）的训练加速**！
   - **底层机理解构——输出词元重叠率（Output-Token Overlap Ratio）与双重在策略性**：
     - 有效蒸馏不仅要求轨迹对**学生**是在策略的，还要求轨迹对**教师**不能过度离策略（On-policyness w.r.t. both student and teacher）；
     - 当初始师生对齐度较低（低 Token 重叠率）时，在线 OPD 随着训练推进和上下文拉长，学生轨迹会越来越偏离教师流形，导致蒸馏信号严重退化；而 `Semi-OPD` 在较短有效上下文窗口上稳定覆盖完整轨迹，使学生暴露于更多教师偏好的高质量 Token 分布下。
4. **对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
   与今日第 8 篇核心论文 `A²-OPD` 共同构成了在策略蒸馏理论的“双子星”：直接给出了何时用在线 `OPD`（高师生 Token 重叠率）、何时用 `Semi-OPD`（低重叠率/跨族迁移，省 11 倍算力且防长度崩溃）的定量判据！

> On-policy distillation (OPD) has become increasingly popular for transferring teacher capabilities to student models. In this work, we ask a critical research question: Is on-policy sampling always beneficial for distilling arbitrary teacher-student pairs? We show that a simple alternative, Semi-OPD, which distills from offline rollouts generated by the initial student, can often outperform OPD in both accuracy and training efficiency. Across 17 teacher-student pairs ranging from 1.5B to 235B parameters, Semi-OPD outperforms OPD in 14 cases, with up to +13.6% accuracy and 11.4x training speedup. We further find that the choice between OPD and Semi-OPD depends on the alignment between the initial teacher and student, quantified by an output-token overlap ratio: OPD is beneficial only when the two are highly aligned with high overlap ratios. Our deeper investigation suggests that effective distillation requires on-policyness w.r.t. both the student and the teacher. For misaligned pairs, student rollouts can become increasingly off-policy w.r.t. the teacher as context length grows, weakening the distillation signal. In contrast, Semi-OPD is often more stable, as it distills on shorter contexts while covering full trajectories and exposing the student to more teacher-preferred tokens. Beyond proposing Semi-OPD as an efficient alternative, our work motivates the community to rethink when to use OPD and to study stronger OPD variants with meaningful teacher-student pairs.

   - **AAArena 真实人类天梯检验**：汇集清华大学智能体大赛历年 **12 款真实复杂对抗游戏与 1,920 份归档人类参赛程序**。评测显示，`Opus 5.5 + Claude Code` 组合在固定对局预算内自主演化并斩获 **6 枚金牌（登顶半数人类天梯）**，同时揭示出在规则耦合极度复杂的长视距游戏中，现有智能体仍难以击败顶尖人类冠军。
4. **对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：

> Adversarial games have driven advances from heuristic search to reinforcement learning, yet learning and adapting strategies from limited samples remain challenging. AI agents offer an alternative by turning game experience into revisions of executable policies. Building on heuristic learning (HL), we formalize Adversarial Heuristic Learning (AHL), a paradigm that uses AI agents as learning engines to refine game policies and supporting software while keeping model weights fixed. We introduce AAArena, a benchmark comprising 12 authentic adversarial games and 1,920 archived human programs, with an evaluation protocol modeled on real-world game competitions. Further experiments show that opponent selection and dense feedback support policy improvement, and that agents learn from both on-policy replays of their own matches and off-policy replays of other players' matches. These results highlight HL's potential in adversarial games and identify persistent challenges in game understanding, strategy implementation, and long-horizon policy development.

## 五、 今日学术终极洞见：从“单点手工作坊”迈向“契约化递归优化与双向状态流” (Ultimate Academic Insights)

纵观今日（`2026-10-12`）全量 41 篇池子中涌现的 **11 篇核心自进化突破**，我们可以清晰地捕捉到自进化智能体研究正在发生的**四大深层范式跃迁**：

### 1. 递归自改进（RSI）与梯度外代码演化的“极简契约统一律” (`RSI-Anything` + `Encore` + `AAArena/AHL`)
今日三篇重磅工作从科研工程、具身机器人与对抗博弈三个维度共同证明了**“可执行软件/策略代码的闭环迭代修补（Learning Beyond Gradients）”**：

### 2. 脚手架与上下文状态的“双向因果重构”：打破单向追加的熵增诅咒 (`SLA` + `ReHarness` + `Video-RSI`)
今日三篇重量级外围架构论文不约而同地向“只增不改、单向累加”的传统有状态上下文宣战：
- **`SLA`（无状态语言智能体）** 在**步级（Step-level）**彻底废除了追加式长轨迹 $h_t$，证明智能体每一轮只应基于上一轮显式重构的紧凑 `Workspace` 独立行动——这不仅让长程推理的上下文长度蜕变为常数 $O(1)$，更彻底消除了多步 RL 训练中的长上下文显存爆炸；
- **`ReHarness`（循环脚手架）** 在**跨样本流（Stream-level）**打破了传统记忆库“只写不改”的单向流，通过 `Backward Revision` 将下游任务的成败作为真实监督信号，逆向回溯修订早期被检索出的过度泛化反思，实现了纯文本空间中的“误差反向传播”；

- **`Semi-OPD`** 则从采样轨迹分布维度证明：有效蒸馏必须同时满足**对学生与对教师的双重在策略性**——当师生初始输出词元重叠率较低时，盲目在线采样学生轨迹反而会随上下文增长严重偏离教师流形，而固定复用初始学生离线轨迹（`Semi-OPD`）在 17 组师生对的 14 组中反超在线 OPD，并带来 11.4 倍训练加速！

### 4. 训练环境的“程序化自修复合成”黄金标准 (`SWE-Duel` + `ProgramGym` + `SoftwareGym`)
