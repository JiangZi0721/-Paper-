### 1. [Memento 3: Model-Based Recursive Self-Improvement through Reflective Rulebooks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11794)

- **论文元数据**：`arXiv:2610.11794` | [Hugging Face 论文讨论页](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.11794) | [arXiv 原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11794)
- **官方 PDF 文献**：**[📑 点击在线阅读官方原版 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.11794.pdf)**
- **学术评级**：⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 【模型基递归自我提升·规则书编译代码世界模型·ARC-AGI 突破】
- **双维定位**：`[慢思考推理与隐式世界模型 (Reasoning / Test-Time)]` × `[测试时/运行态自演进 (Runtime Evolution)]`
- **研究团队与机构**：Haoyu Zhao, Zhengxu Yu, Zhiyuan He, Meng Fang, Rasul Tutunov 等（University College London / UCL）
- **核心自进化流派**：基于外部符号记忆的模型基递归自我提升（Model-Based RSI）、代码即模型（Code-as-Model）

#### 🔬 深度科研结构化拆解：
1. **具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：
   研究在模型权重被冻结（Frozen LLM）的严苛约束下，智能体如何在进入陌生且规则未知的动态环境时，不依赖任何微调，自主归纳环境交互动力学，并实现持续的**模型基递归自我提升（Model-Based RSI）**。
2. **解决了什么核心痛点 (Problem Solved & Pain Points)**：
   以往免训练自演化方案存在两大瓶颈：
   - **世界模型多义性（Underspecified Hypotheses）**：有限的单步观察往往同时兼容多种相互矛盾的动力学假设，纯自然语言反思无法精确回放与验证；
   - **规划与执行脱节**：纯文本生成的反思策略往往难以量化为严密的动作转移步长，容易出现因果幻觉与累积漂移。
3. **提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
   提出 **Memento 3**，确立了“反思规则书 $\to$ 可执行代码模型”的递归自闭环：
   - **自然语言规则书（Reflective Rulebook）**：作为持久语义记忆，记录针对环境动态的可修正假设；
   - **代码编译与双重验证门禁（Compilation & Dual Verification）**：自动将规则书编译为可运行的 Python 预测与规划代码。更新的代码必须通过双重门禁：① 规则书语义忠实度校验；② 单元格级精确历史回放（Cell-exact replay），严格复现已观测转移；
   - **种群级多模型协同（Population Extension）**：并行维护多个世界模型假设，共享交互证据，利用模型间分歧主动引导好奇心探索；
   - **实测表现震撼**：在严苛抽象推理基准 **ARC-AGI-3 上清空全部 25 个公开游戏关卡**，相对人类动作效率（RHAE）达到 100.0，仅消耗 44% 人类步数；在 Atari Pong 测试中，自演化出的反馈控制器以 21:0 零封基线，且测试时零外部 LLM 调用。
4. **对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
   向学术界证明：**递归自我提升完全不必依赖高成本的权重反向传播**。通过将认知世界模型编译为确定性可执行代码，并在交互误差反馈下实现代码的自主重构与验证，智能体即可获得极强且可迁移的通用问题解决能力。

<details><summary>👉 点击展开查看论文官方英文 Abstract 原文</summary>

> Learning to act in unfamiliar environments requires agents to infer how the world works and revise that understanding as new evidence arrives. Yet limited observations can support multiple world models that explain past interactions but predict different outcomes in unseen states. We introduce Memento 3, building on the Memento series to enable frozen LLM agents to continually learn explicit world models through external memory. The agent maintains a natural-language rulebook as persistent semantic memory, recording revisable hypotheses about environment dynamics while leaving unknown aspects underspecified. It compiles this rulebook into executable code for prediction and planning. Through a continual loop of observation, reflection, rule revision, compilation, and verification, the agent uses prediction errors to refine both the rulebook and its code. Updated code is accepted only when the LLM judges it faithful to the rulebook and cell-exact replay reproduces the observed transitions. We investigate this process as a model-based route to recursive self-improvement (RSI): the agent autonomously explores the environment, revises its world model, and uses verified updates to guide subsequent interaction and learning, while the underlying LLM remains fixed. A population extension maintains multiple world models in parallel, sharing interaction evidence and using their predictions to guide exploration. On ARC-AGI-3, the single-model agent clears every level of all 25 public games, achieves a mean Relative Human Action Efficiency (RHAE) of 100.0, and uses 44% of the human action count. In an Atari Pong case study, a learned feedback controller wins 21:0 in each of three evaluated episodes with different openings, without further LLM calls.

</details>

### 2. [AgentGarten: Code Worlds for Evolving Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12374)

- **论文元数据**：`arXiv:2610.12374` | [Hugging Face 论文讨论页](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.12374) | [arXiv 原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12374) | [💻 官方开源代码](https://github.com/MirroS-Lab/AgentGarten)
- **官方 PDF 文献**：**[📑 点击在线阅读官方原版 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.12374.pdf)**
- **学术评级**：⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 【代码世界自进化环境·对抗强迫微分回放·跨代战术手册继承】
- **双维定位**：`[环境交互与自适应沙盒 (Act / Tool-Use)]` × `[系统元设计阶段 (Meta-Design / Search)]`
- **研究团队与机构**：Jiawei Chi, Shangchen Miao, Zhiyuan Shi, Kailu Wu, Hanyang Wang 等（MirroS-Lab）
- **核心自进化流派**：环境与沙盒自主代码合成（Environment Synthesis / Code Worlds）、战术手册跨世代蒸馏演进

#### 🔬 深度科研结构化拆解：
1. **具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：
   研究如何突破自进化智能体训练中的“环境多样性瓶颈”，构建一个能够让智能体以代码形式自主编写扩展世界规则、同时提供逼真视觉渲染的高保真交互沙盒系统。
2. **解决了什么核心痛点 (Problem Solved & Pain Points)**：
   以往智能体训练沙盒要么是规则固定不可修改的硬编码模拟器，要么是缺乏确定性物理状态与因果一致性的纯扩散生成视频。智能体无法在保持动力学严密性的同时，自主合成新任务。
3. **提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
   提出 **AgentGarten** 框架，实现计算仿真与神经渲染的解耦共演：
   - **代码世界（Code Worlds）接口**：后端维护持久世界状态，所有交互规则均由程序代码定义，支持通过代码动态生成具有阶梯难度的新关卡；
   - **对抗强迫神经渲染（Adversarial Forcing）**：通过精确回放使得历史 Prefilling 全程可微，未来时间步的预测损失能够反向更新渲染器对历史状态的编码，达到实时的视觉渲染与因果一致性；
   - **跨代战术手册继承（Cross-Generation Playbook Distillation）**：智能体将每轮成功的探索轨迹提炼为结构化“战术手册（Playbook）”，后续世代的智能体直接在此基础上继承并精炼，**仅用 4 轮交互即达到了传统强化学习上百万次采样的收敛效果**。
4. **对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
   AgentGarten 为学术界展示了“代码定义世界、经验沉淀手册”的宏大图景，使“环境复杂度随智能体能力同步自演化（Co-Evolution of Agent and World）”成为可能。

<details><summary>👉 点击展开查看论文官方英文 Abstract 原文</summary>

> Interactive virtual worlds allow agents to learn through exploration and interaction. What agents can learn is bounded by the environments they practice in, which must be faithful, with consistent state, rules, and dynamics, and realistic, with observations that follow the real-world visual distributions. Achieving both across diverse worlds remains a bottleneck. We introduce AgentGarten, a framework that couples simulators and game engines with a shared neural renderer to build real-time interactive environments. Its simulation backends maintain persistent world state and execute program-defined interaction rules, while the renderer generates visual observations from structured conditions exported through a common interface. To build the neural renderer, we adapt a pretrained video model to geometry conditions, distill it with our proposed Adversarial Forcing, and optimize inference for real-time interaction. Adversarial Forcing makes history prefilling differentiable through exact replay, so that losses on later predictions update how the renderer encodes prior observations, and adds real-data adversarial supervision to improve its visual quality. In AgentGarten, agents perceive the world through visual observations, interact with it in real time, and improve by distilling each round of experience into playbooks that subsequent agents inherit and refine. Our empirical study demonstrates a substantial gain in learning efficiency, with agents learning from just 4 rounds compared with millions for a conventional reinforcement learning counterpart. As new worlds can be written as code and rendered through the same interface, environments can scale in both number and difficulty alongside their agents, a step toward agents that keep evolving through interactive experience.

</details>

#### 🔬 深度科研结构化拆解：
1. **具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：
   研究在多模态视觉语言模型（VLM）智能体中，如何摆脱文本线性化转换对空间几何信息的破坏，构建能够直接表征视觉交互并与强化学习策略共同自演化的视觉原生技能系统。

1. **具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：

   提出 **Opera** 框架，确立“便签式持续追踪与在策略回流”机制：
   - **持久问题便签（Persistent Notes）**：每一条修正意见均作为未结项便签，结合事件驱动算子，持续审查后续代码提交是否真正消除了根因；

> Long-horizon coding agents need timely corrections, yet feedback can be ineffective or even harmful when it misjudges ongoing work or fails to address the underlying problem. Existing critics focus on evaluating trajectories and generating feedback, but rarely track what happens after feedback is delivered. We present Opera, a verbal critic framework that treats each correction as a persistent note, followed until the diagnosed problem is resolved. Opera decides when to review through periodic and event-driven triggers, diagnoses issues with typed operators, audits feedback against visible evidence before delivery, and tracks the agent's subsequent actions to distinguish mere compliance from actual resolution. As a test-time critic, Opera improves the resolve rate of non-critic agents by up to 12.4, 15.0, and 8.9 percentage points on Terminal-Bench 2.1, a SWE-Bench Pro subset, and DeepSWE v1.1, respectively, across four policy models, and achieves the highest mean resolve rate among competitive critic baselines on all three benchmarks, and also improves policy models when the policy critiques itself. Our code is available at: https://github.com/dongyuanjushi/Opera.

## 五、 🧠 终极学术洞察与自进化技术趋势研判

1. **递归自我提升（RSI）向“代码即模型（Code-as-Model）”全面升维**：
   `Memento 3` 与 `Embodied Turing Machines (COAP)` 共同昭示了一个极为关键的技术跃迁：**自进化的最高效载体不是庞大脆弱的神经网络权重，而是确定性、可版本管理、可单元回放测试的纯代码**。大模型充当“编译器与架构师”，将归纳出的环境动力学编译为显式代码；一旦代码生成，执行速度飙升千百倍，且能实现跨任务的稳健积累。
2. **失败信息的因果再利用重塑演化算力效率**：
   `Mara Chain` 对传统“丢弃失败候选”范式的反思，直击搜索与自演化算法的底层浪费根源。将失败轨迹视为信息丰富的负向探针，结合链式定向精炼，使演化探索的样本利用率发生质的飞跃（Rollout 开销暴降 65.5%）。
3. **强化学习策略优化（RLVR）向序列非对称核函数深化**：
   `ReSPO` 揭示了在策略/离策略数据复用中，经典 PPO/GRPO 截断机制对“罕见长思维链正样本”的梯度灭杀效应。通过平滑双分支核函数对冲重要度权重的长尾抖动，为长程多轮自进化训练提供了关键的数学动力学保障。
