### 1. [Scaling Trajectories for Complex Tasks through Recursive Self-Rewrite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02826)

- **学术评级**：⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ [跨 Harness 经验重构·递归自重写 (RSR)·沙盒去偏自蒸馏]
- **论文元数据**：`arXiv:2610.02826` | [Hugging Face 讨论页](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.02826) | [arXiv 原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02826)
- **📑 本地 PDF 全文**：**[点击直接在本地打开论文 PDF](./2610.02826_Scaling Trajectories for Complex Tasks through Recursiv.pdf)** *(已归档至本目录)*
- **作者与机构**：Zongxia Li, Yucheng Shi, Zhongzhi Li 等（Tencent Hunyuan）
- **社区关注度**：🔥 **10** Upvotes

#### 🔬 深度科研结构化拆解：

- **1. 具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：
  探索如何将复杂异构外部脚手架（Heterogeneous Scaffolds / Specialized Harnesses）在长程任务中探索发现的成功解题经验，转化为通用单体智能体在基础轻量 Harness 下即可自主执行的高质量 SFT 训练轨迹。
- **2. 解决了什么核心痛点 (Problem Solved & Pain Points)**：
  部署态与训练态存在严重的“脚手架鸿沟（Scaffolding Gap）”。离线探索复杂长程任务时，通常需要挂载重型专用脚手架（如复杂树搜索、多步专用规划器、特权诊断工具）；但智能体在实际部署时无法承受昂贵的多脚手架开销。若直接在多脚手架探索轨迹上做 SFT，会把脚手架干预甚至验证器线索/答案泄露（Solution Leakage）生搬硬套进模型权重，导致过拟合与部署失效。
- **3. 提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
  提出“递归自重写（Recursive Self-Rewrite, RSR）”框架：
1. **Planner（规程提纯）**：从不同异构 Harness 探索出的成功轨迹中提取出纯净的通用执行规程（Runbooks）；
2. **Critic（递归合规性审查）**：深度扫描规程，彻底剔除验证器线索、答案泄露与脚手架特权痕迹，驱动递归修正；
3. **Executor（隔离沙盒全新重跑）**：驱动单一基础模型在完全隔离、无特权泄露的全新沙盒中严格复现该 Runbook，生成标准格式的端到端执行轨迹；
4. 将 2,001 条高难度源轨迹递归扩增重构为 11,094 条通用沙盒训练轨迹。
- **4. 对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
  在 Terminal-Bench 2 (pass@3 从 57.0% 提升至 74.2%)、Terminal-Bench 4 (1.5% 暴涨至 9.1%)、Terminal-Bench Hard (39.0% 跃升至 63.0%) 上展现出极强的泛化提升，打通了“外部多脚手架强化探索”到“单体智能体参数内化”的自进化闭环通道。

<details><summary>👉 点击展开查看论文官方英文 Abstract 原文</summary>

> Successful trajectories on difficult tasks provide valuable supervision for model improvement, but specialized harnesses introduce interventions that may be unavailable during deployment. We propose Recursive Self-Rewrite (RSR), a framework that uses one base model, Qwen-3.8-27B, to discover successful solutions under diverse harnesses and reconstruct them as training trajectories under a general harness. A planner extracts procedures into runbooks, a critic screens for verifier and solution leakage and guides recursive revision, and an executor follows qualified runbooks in fresh sandboxes. Across approximately 3K self-curated terminal tasks, three harnesses jointly solve 759 tasks, 34.3% more than the strongest individual harness in the recorded pool. RSR expands 2,001 successful source trajectories into 11,094 rewritten trajectories for supervised finetuning. Training on these trajectories outperforms both the base model and direct trajectory SFT. Compared with the base model, pass@3 increases from 57.0% to 74.2% on Terminal-Bench 2, from 1.5% to 9.1% on Terminal-Bench 4, from 39.0% to 63.0% on our self-curated Terminal-Bench Hard, and from 3.0% to 6.0% on our Software Terminal-Bench. Process reward on Long-Horizon Terminal-Bench rises from 0.21 to 0.29. These results show how diverse harness-assisted experiences can be reconstructed into reusable capabilities for a model operating under a general harness.

</details>

### 2. [Recursive Harness Self-Improvement for Frontier Reasoning Data Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03548)

- **学术评级**：⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ [任务-脚手架双闭环共演·递归脚手架自演化 (RSI)·动态难度数据自举]
- **论文元数据**：`arXiv:2610.03548` | [Hugging Face 讨论页](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.03548) | [arXiv 原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03548)
- **📑 本地 PDF 全文**：**[点击直接在本地打开论文 PDF](./2610.03548_Recursive Harness Self-Improvement for Frontier Reasoni.pdf)** *(已归档至本目录)*
- **作者与机构**：Wenlong Zhang, Zhengbo Jiao, Chenxu Zhang 等（前沿推理实验室 / 基础模型对齐小组）
- **社区关注度**：🆕 arXiv 最新发布

#### 🔬 深度科研结构化拆解：

- **1. 具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：
  研究面向前沿长程推理与代码合成中，如何让生成任务的外部合成脚手架（Synthesis Harness）与任务难度分布同步演进，避免数据生成过程中的边际收益衰减与能力停滞。
- **2. 解决了什么核心痛点 (Problem Solved & Pain Points)**：
  现有的任务递归合成仅在任务层面复用 Seed 问题（Task-level Recursion），而底层的脚手架代码、Prompt 和工具流是静态固定的。随着学生模型能力提升，静态 Harness 生成的问题迅速被全部解决（准确率达到 100%），无法持续施加有效的前沿挑战压力（Diminishing Returns）。
- **3. 提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
  提出“任务-脚手架协同演化（Task-Harness Co-Evolution）”与“递归脚手架自改进（Recursive Harness Self-Improvement, RSI）”：
1. **在线自演化（Online Self-Improvement）**：在生成过程中，将求解器（Solver）中途出现的失败轨迹与报错即时编译提纯为可复用的结构化技能（Reusable Skills）；
2. **批次后元演进（Post-Task Meta-Improvement）**：每个合成 Batch 结束后，自动化元算法对 Harness 的 Prompt、技能库和调度流进行变异搜索，只有当变异后的 Harness 能够在受控成本下生成“解法正确但让基线求解器感到更难”的任务时，才正式吸收该 Harness 候选版本；
3. 历经 14 轮协同演进，基线求解器的解题率从 100% 被系统性压制到 54.8%，成功合成了极具挑战性的前沿数学与代码数据。
- **4. 对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
  27B 学生模型经此合成的 10K 数学数据微调后，在 APEX 上斩获 62.5% mean-16，逼近顶级商业闭源模型，证明了脚手架自演化与环境自适应加压能够以极低算力自举出顶级推理数据。

<details><summary>👉 点击展开查看论文官方英文 Abstract 原文</summary>

> Generating progressively harder reasoning problems requires synthesis procedures that adapt as the task distribution evolves. Existing task-level recursion reuses generated problems as seeds but leaves the construction harness unchanged. We present task-harness co-evolution, a framework for recursive harness self-improvement (RSI) in reasoning-data synthesis. Online self-improvement converts intermediate solver failures into reusable skills during generation. Post-task self-improvement revises skills, prompts, and workflows after each batch, adopting candidates only when they generate harder valid tasks within a bounded cost increase. Model weights and verification criteria remain fixed. Across mathematics, coding, and science, mean solver accuracy decreases from 100.0% to 54.8% over fourteen evolution rounds. Ablations show that combining both update schedules produces harder tasks than fixed-harness recursion or either schedule alone. The resulting data improves downstream SFT and GRPO performance. In particular, a 27B student fine-tuned on 10K synthesized mathematics examples achieves 62.5% mean-16 accuracy on APEX, competitive with selected frontier-model references. These results support adapting the synthesis harness alongside the tasks to generate increasingly challenging data with downstream training value.

</details>

### 3. [Credit Where It Matters: Dependency-Aware Policy Optimization for Terminal Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03634)

- **学术评级**：⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ [终端多步强化学习·因果依赖图反向追溯·依赖感知策略优化 (DepGPO)]
- **论文元数据**：`arXiv:2610.03634` | [Hugging Face 讨论页](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.03634) | [arXiv 原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03634)
- **📑 本地 PDF 全文**：**[点击直接在本地打开论文 PDF](./2610.03634_Credit Where It Matters_ Dependency-Aware Policy Optimi.pdf)** *(已归档至本目录)*
- **作者与机构**：Yu Li, Guangfeng Cai, Long-Fei Li 等（清华大学 / 智能体系统联合团队）
- **社区关注度**：🆕 arXiv 最新发布

#### 🔬 深度科研结构化拆解：

- **1. 具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：
  研究长程终端命令行智能体（Terminal Agents）在多步交互强化学习中，信用分配（Credit Assignment）严重失真、无关操作稀释有效梯度的核心难题。
- **2. 解决了什么核心痛点 (Problem Solved & Pain Points)**：
  在复杂的终端任务（如复杂代码编译、环境配置、长程 Debug）中，执行流往往包含几十步 Bash 命令。传统的 Trajectory-level（整条轨迹均摊）或 Step-level（单步等距折扣）奖励分配，无法识别不同命令之间的“读写因果依赖”。智能体往往执行了许多试探性或无关的只读命令（如 ls, pwd, 错误的 cat），最终由几个关键的写操作（如修改配置文件、安装特定补丁）使 Verifier 判定成功。传统 GRPO 将奖励无脑平摊给所有步骤，导致无效冗余操作被盲目强化，引发策略震荡。
- **3. 提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
  提出“依赖感知组策略优化（Dependency-Aware Group Policy Optimization, DepGPO）”：
1. **命令因果依赖图构建**：在沙盒执行期间，通过系统级追踪捕获每条终端命令的底层文件/资源 Read-Write 依赖关系，构建 DAG 图；
2. **Verifier 逆向追溯**：从最终任务验证器（Task Verifier）所检查的核心目标资源节点出发，沿着 DAG 图逆向反向追溯因果路径；
3. **因果权重重加权**：仅对处于因果路径上的核心写操作及其直接依赖的读操作赋予正向信用权重，并以此重新分配整条轨迹在各个决策步上的 Advantage；
4. 结合 GRPO 进行稳健策略更新，彻底过滤掉脱离因果链的冗余探索噪音。
- **4. 对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
  首次将“外部 Verifier 确定性验证”与“系统级底层读写依赖分析”深度融合进 RLVR 梯度更新中，彻底消除了终端智能体强化学习中的信用分配稀释问题，为解决长程代码与系统级智能体自进化提供了关键算法基石。

<details><summary>👉 点击展开查看论文官方英文 Abstract 原文</summary>

> Terminal-using agents benefit from reinforcement learning (RL) in coding, debugging, and other multi-step terminal tasks. In these tasks, later commands often depend on information or intermediate results produced by earlier commands. However, existing trajectory-level and step-level credit assignment methods do not explicitly trace the read-write dependencies through which commands affect the final outcome. Consequently, training signals could still be assigned to irrelevant operations, weakening learning from relevant steps. In this paper, we propose Dependency-Aware Group Policy Optimization (DepGPO), which uses execution dependencies between commands to guide credit assignment for terminal agents. Specifically, we construct a command dependency graph from execution traces and trace backward from the resources inspected by the task verifier. We then assign credit to relevant writes and their supporting reads along these paths, and use it to redistribute trajectory advantages across steps. Extensive comparative experiments and ablation studies demonstrate that DepGPO improves task performance and training stability on complex terminal tasks.

</details>

### 4. [MobiAgent: Dual-Loop Recursive Policy Self-Improvement for Long-Horizon Mobile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03476)

- **学术评级**：⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ [具身自进化·双闭环递归自演进·无监督技能发现与终身微调]
- **论文元数据**：`arXiv:2610.03476` | [Hugging Face 讨论页](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.03476) | [arXiv 原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03476)
- **📑 本地 PDF 全文**：**[点击直接在本地打开论文 PDF](./2610.03476_MobiAgent_ Dual-Loop Recursive Policy Self-Improvement .pdf)** *(已归档至本目录)*
- **作者与机构**：Chenzhi Liu, Yue Zhang, Jiehong Lin 等（具身智能与机器人前沿团队）
- **社区关注度**：🆕 arXiv 最新发布

#### 🔬 深度科研结构化拆解：

- **1. 具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：
  研究长程移动操作机器人（Mobile Manipulation）在复合长程目标下的执行错误累积、高层推理与低层控制容量干扰难题，以及如何实现零人工标注的终身策略自进化。
- **2. 解决了什么核心痛点 (Problem Solved & Pain Points)**：
  传统具身 VLA 模型在长程任务中容易陷入死循环，高层语义重规划缺乏灵活性，且无法从部署阶段积累的失败与成功执行中持续学习，缺乏自主闭环更新机制。
- **3. 提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
  提出“双闭环递归策略自改进（Dual-Loop Recursive Policy Self-Improvement）”架构：
1. **内闭环（Inner Loop - 部署执行）**：采用滚动时域规划（Receding-Horizon Planning）与视觉反思机制，将高层 VLM 决策解耦为高度可组合的原子技能，由共享统一骨干的流匹配（Flow-Matching）专家并行执行，消除能力干扰；
2. **外闭环（Outer Loop - 终身递归自演化）**：自主收集机器人部署回滚轨迹，自主对多轮轨迹进行片段切分（Segmentation）与确定性环境结果验证，聚类发现新型高价值原子技能，并持续自动微调技能库；
3. 在 RoboCasa、BEHAVIOR-1K 与真机 Astribot S1 上全程无需人类干预，通过数据自循环使任务成功率分别从 7.5% 跃升至 27.5%、从 32.5% 跃升至 57.5%。
- **4. 对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
  完美构建了具身智能体的“运行时执行反思（内环）+ 离线经验无监督聚类自进化（外环）”双闭环动力学，为具身系统脱离人工示教走向终身自主学习指明了可行工程路径。

<details><summary>👉 点击展开查看论文官方英文 Abstract 原文</summary>

> Long-horizon mobile manipulation presents significant challenges due to compounding execution errors and capacity interference between locomotion and arm control. While recent Vision-Language-Action models excel at short-horizon tasks, they lack the hierarchical reasoning required for multi-stage objectives. Furthermore, existing hierarchical agents suffer from rigid sub-task mapping, inflexible replanning, and a lack of continuous learning. To address these limitations, we introduce MobiAgent, a dual-loop agentic framework that bridges robust deployment execution and recursive policy self-improvement. During deployment, the Inner Loop decouples high-level reasoning from low-level control through highly composable atomic skills. It employs Vision-Language models for receding-horizon planning and visual reflection, dynamically composing skills to ensure robust error recovery. These skills are executed by specialized flow-matching experts that share a unified VLM backbone, maximizing reusability while mitigating capacity interference. Concurrently, the Outer Loop drives automated lifelong learning by autonomously segmenting and verifying deployment rollouts, clustering them to discover atomic skills, and continuously fine-tuning the skill library without human annotations. Evaluations on RoboCasa, BEHAVIOR-1K, and real-world tasks demonstrate the effectiveness of MobiAgent. It outperforms $_{0.5}$-TA by 22.5 percentage points on BEHAVIOR-1K and enables robust recovery from execution failures. Through autonomous data recycling, success improves from 7.50% to 27.50% on RoboCasa and from 32.5% to 57.5% on Astribot S1.

### 5. [MetaRubric: Learning to Reward for Rubric-Based Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02824)

- **学术评级**：⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ [RLVR 奖励作弊对冲·细粒度量规共演·空洞信用 (Vacuous Credit) 消除]
- **论文元数据**：`arXiv:2610.02824` | [Hugging Face 讨论页](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.02824) | [arXiv 原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02824)
- **📑 本地 PDF 全文**：**[点击直接在本地打开论文 PDF](./2610.02824_MetaRubric_ Learning to Reward for Rubric-Based Reinfor.pdf)** *(已归档至本目录)*
- **作者与机构**：Yuxuan Fan, Jaehong Yoon（Nanyang Technological University (NTU Singapore)）
- **社区关注度**：🔥 **3** Upvotes

#### 🔬 深度科研结构化拆解：

- **1. 具体研究什么 (What is being studied)**：
  研究基于分面量规（Rubric-based）的强化学习中，如何消除判决器产生的虚假评分，实现策略优化与量规验证标准的协同自适应。
- **2. 解决了什么核心痛点 (Problem Solved & Pain Points)**：
  基于量规（Rubric-based）的强化学习常用于开放式长程任务，但判决器极易产生“空洞信用（Vacuous Credit）”——即即使智能体完全没有输出某条准则所需的动作或关键事实，判决器仍然给出高分。这种虚假给分即使在剔除相关信息后依然存在，甚至导致 GRPO Advantage 的正负号完全颠倒，严重破坏策略训练。
- **3. 提出的新技术 / 新架构 / 新理念 (Novel Techniques & Concepts)**：
  提出“MetaRubric 策略-量规协同演进框架”：
1. **反事实事实干预（Counterfactual Fact Intervention）**：对每个 Prompt 变换单点关键事实，生成反事实对照组，确保仅在响应包含充分确定性支撑证据时才赋予信用；
2. **策略响应引导的量规自适应（Response-Guided Rubric Adaptation）**：在每次策略优化阶段结束后，利用当前策略的实际响应动态修正原始和反事实量规标准，并在阶段边界根据策略高发错误自适应调整不同准则的权重；
3. 在 PubMedQA 和 HealthBench-Hard 上相比静态判决器 GRPO 提升高达 6.00~20.40 个百分点，彻底杜绝了虚假奖励欺骗。
- **4. 对自进化智能体研究的深远影响与启示 (Impact on Self-Evolving Agents)**：
  解决了大模型在没有完全确定性单元测试的复杂语义环境中开展 RL 自进化时的关键难题，将奖励建模从“静态黑盒裁判”转变为“伴随策略动态对抗演进的证据验证器”。

<details><summary>👉 点击展开查看论文官方英文 Abstract 原文</summary>

> Rubric-based reinforcement learning extends reward-driven optimization to open-ended tasks by assigning partial credit to individual response requirements. However, rubric judges can assign a high criterion score even when the information or action it requires is absent from the response, a failure mode we term Vacuous Credit. Such awards persist after the required information is removed and can reverse the sign of a response's GRPO advantage. To address this problem, we introduce MetaRubric, which alternates evidence-aware policy optimization with response-guided rubric adaptation. We construct counterfactual counterparts by changing one task-relevant fact in each prompt. During policy optimization, credit is assigned only when the response contains sufficient evidence to satisfy the required rubric criterion. After each policy-optimization stage, current policy responses guide revisions to original and counterfactual criteria while preserving the meaning of the original prompt's initial rubric as interpreted under each prompt's facts. We also adapt criterion weights at stage boundaries to better address observed policy errors. Across multiple backbones, MetaRubric improves PubMedQA accuracy by 6.00--20.40 percentage points over static-judge GRPO, with further gains on HealthBench-Hard and two multimodal medical benchmarks.
